Part 2:
What values will guide your team?
	We hold accountability among each other very highly. Respect and honesty between each groupmate is expected and feedback is welcomed. Inclusivity is required as we want to include our members and listen to each other attentively.

How often will you meet?
	We have decided to meet twice a week. 

When and where will you meet?
	We will meet during and after our lab section, in Arts 204. Tuesdays 4-6pm after our lab, and Thursdays from 4-6pm. 

What happens if someone cannot attend?
	If someone cannot attend, we will try to fill the person in on any details they missed. We can provide them with a text document on what they missed, along with a brief explanation in the next meeting.

What communication tools will you use?
	We have decided to use discord to communicate with each other. It was the one platform that we all had in common. 

Which tool will be used for urgent matters?
	We have exchanged discord. If any urgent matters appear, we may call each other over discord, although we all are good at responding in a timely manner. 

How will important information be shared with the entire team?
	Important information will be shared over GitHub, and email, and meetings. This will be able to reach the entire team. 

How quickly should team members respond to messages?
	Messages should be responded to as soon as possible. Leeway is given, but after 24 hours it will be considered unacceptable. 

What should someone do if they cannot respond?
	If a team member is in a state where they can't respond, they should try to plan their situation beforehand with the team. If they cannot plan their situation beforehand, they may explain their situation after the fact. 

What happens if a member repeatedly fails to respond?
	The group can discuss the resulting actions and vote unanimously on the final decision.

What does respectful behaviour mean to your team?
	We expect respectful language throughout our meetings, talking accountability on your own work, and showing up to meetings.

What behaviours are unacceptable?
	Insulting one another is unacceptable. Not contributing equally is unacceptable. Missing multiple meetings without notice is unacceptable. 

How will you make sure everyone has an opportunity to participate?
	In our meetings, we will make sure that every piece of the project is evenly distributed. Each member will have an opportunity to participate as everyone has assigned tasks.

What will your team do when a member does not meet the expectations agreed upon by the team?
	We will make a note about it, but we will try to prevent that with constant reminders and meetings to update each other. If a team member fails to constantly meet expectations, we will address it directly as a group in person.

How will your team make decisions?
	Decisions will be made together in meetings, or through discord communications. In meetings, we can form agreements based on a voting system, or by using the wheel. 

How will your team handle disagreements?
	In the event of an equal number of votes being counted upon opposing perspectives, a coin can be tossed and the result must be respected by everyone in the group.

Identify potential challenges your team may face and explain how you will address them.
	We could face challenges in scheduling, as some may face more workload with other courses in specific weeks/days. Although we are confident in our abilities, we may face merge conflicts, or other issues in GitHub.
